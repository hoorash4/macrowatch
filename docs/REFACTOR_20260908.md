undo a deployment; the matching deployment must also finish successfully.

## Preservation decisions

- Keep old_version: previously published pages/URLs and historical artifacts are not proven unused.
- Keep migrations and endpoint directories; their names and order are operational contracts.
- Keep distinct earnings aggregation, backfill and automatic policies, including null/zero handling.
- Keep tracker state in one closure: its drag/edit/modal lifecycle shares state; separate files would add coupling.
- Keep feature chart renderers separate even where visually similar; their axes and provisional rules differ.
- Do not merge the two HTML escaping policies: their apostrophe/null behavior differs.
